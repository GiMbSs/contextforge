# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

ContextForge contributors, maintainers, and participants are committed to a
welcoming, safe, and respectful community for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience, ability, appearance, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate constructively and with empathy.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, boundaries, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Prioritize community safety and the project's public interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern.
- Any conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a repository maintainer. Do not use public
issues when doing so would expose a participant or sensitive details.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where practical,
and respond proportionally. Actions may include a private correction, warning,
temporary participation restriction, removal of content, or permanent exclusion.
Anyone directly involved in a report must recuse themselves from its resolution.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents
ContextForge.
